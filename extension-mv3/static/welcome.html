Two things it does

Save

Adds the article to your Screenbreak account, ready for your next booklet.

Print

Opens a clean version to print at home, or to keep as a PDF. No account needed.

When you click the button

You can change this any time in Settings, or by right-clicking the button.

Every article you print also goes to your Screenbreak account. Needs a login.

Without the button

Use a shortcut, or right-click anywhere on the page.
